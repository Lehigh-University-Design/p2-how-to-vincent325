Planning Your Trip

Check Your Route

The NYC Subway runs 24/7 across 472 stations on 25 routes with 665 miles of track. Before heading out, use the MTA app or Google Maps to plan your journey. Express trains skip stations, so check if you need local or express service.

Quick Tips:

  • Check for delays or service changes. Usually posted on the MTA website or Google Maps
  • Check weekend service changes on mta.info
  • The MTA map is available at most stations and on train cars if needed

The MTA app shows real-time arrivals

Payment Methods

Paying for the subway with OMNY

Ways to Pay

The fare is $2.90 per ride. You can pay using OMNY (tap your phone or contactless card) or a MetroCard. OMNY caps weekly fares at $34 after 12 rides.

Payment Options:

  • OMNY: Tap your phone or contactless card on the reader
  • MetroCard: 7-day unlimited for $34, 30-day for $132
  • Free transfers: One free transfer between subway and bus within 2 hours

Navigating the Station

Finding Your Way

Look for train line indicators (numbers or letters in colored circles) and directional signs. Pay attention to whether you need Uptown (north) or Downtown (south) before you swipe in.

Navigation Tips:

  • Platform direction: Uptown/Bronx goes north, Downtown/Brooklyn goes south
  • Express vs Local: Express (diamond) skips stations, Local (circle) stops at all
  • Transfers: Look for "Transfer" signs to connect between lines
  • Digital boards: Show arrival times and service alerts

Follow the signs to find your platform

Be a Good Rider

The subway moves 5.5 million people daily. Move to the center of the car, let people exit before boarding, and keep belongings close. Don't force closing doors.

Etiquette & Safety:

  • Priority seating: Give your seat to elderly, pregnant, or disabled riders
  • Stay alert: Keep valuables secure and be aware of surroundings
